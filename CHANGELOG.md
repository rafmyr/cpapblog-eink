# Changelog

The history of versions before this repository is not published. The specification in this repository is version 1.2 ([docs/spec/](docs/spec/README.md)); the first tag is `spec-v1.2` together with `v1.0.0-beta.1`.

## Unreleased

### Added

- Night portrait S11 (`Portret nocy`, [ADR-0040](docs/decisions/0040-portret-nocy.md), spec 5.4.16, R-POR-01…R-POR-27): when the last night was computed from the session files (AirBridge 2.3.1 or newer), the first morning screen draws it on a 12-hour clock face, a ninth visualisation form "ring with peaks". The black ring is time with mask (gaps are breaks), white segments are leak, peaks outwards are events in 5 minutes (CPAP, AutoSet) or higher pressure support than usual (ASV, ASVAuto, PS at least 2 cmH2O above the norm median), peaks inwards are central events (CPAP, AutoSet), dots are single events (ASV). A mark at the start of the 12-hour window (a radial line through the ring with an arrowhead in the reading direction) replaces a time range in the middle of the face, which was read as hours of sleep; the centre of the face stays empty. Beside the face: the night numbers (`{time} z maską · AHI {x}`), up to 4 captions (the norm sentence first, then `Najgęściej {hh:mm}–{hh:mm}`, `Wyciek przez {n} min`, breaks, central events, `Ciśnienie na maksimum {n} min` in AutoSet and `EPAP na maksimum {n} min` in ASVAuto, `Drzemka poza tarczą`) and a legend with only the marks that are drawn. Captions only describe the night (ADR-0002); a missing measurement always gets a caption before the others (`Wsparcie oddechu: uczę się.`, `Wyciek: brak odczytu.`, `Zdarzenia: brak odczytu.`, `Rodzaj bezdechów: brak zapisu.`, `Maksimum ciśnienia: nieznane.`), and when there are more of them than places they are combined in one caption `Nieznane: {lista}.` in at most 2 lines, so a missing measurement never drops out. The winner of the morning groups R5e–R9 comes as the second morning screen after 30 min; on mornings with a problem screen (R2–R5d), and on the second morning screen, the portrait is a page under ▶ before K1 until the day slot starts. The panel computes 5-minute segments of the last night in the same pass over the `PLD` samples and `EVE` events as the night itself and keeps them in `/dane/portret.csv` (format `portret` version 1, about 4–19 KB, last night only, in the card backup and the file dump); no extra AirBridge requests. Without session files (older AirBridge, JSON report) the morning is as before. Code `src/portret.cpp`, `src/portret_ekran.cpp`; reference generator in Python `tools/portret/` with a synthetic card (`karta_portretu.py`); tests `test/testy_portret.cpp`, `tools/portret/test_portret.py`, previews `s11-*` from `tools/prototyp/testy.sh`.
- Alerts for deviations from the personal norm ([ADR-0039](docs/decisions/0039-alerty-odchylen-od-normy.md), spec 5.8.13, R-KON-64…R-KON-68): every metric with a norm that had no morning card of its own now gets one when it is in a deviation state (at least 3 of the last 5 valid nights outside the limit and at least Δ from the norm median). Eight new cards S-K-40…S-K-47: pressure support higher than usual (M-28, ASV and ASVAuto only), minute ventilation higher and lower (M-15), tidal volume lower and higher (M-14), breathing rate higher (M-13, not on a morning with S-K-30 or S-K-17 and not during an infection period), more periodic breathing (M-09 as % of the night, ASV and ASVAuto only) and more flow limitation (M-10 P95). Each card shows the night value and the usual value, a 14-night chart with the norm line and one sentence on common ordinary causes, without diagnosis and without device settings (ADR-0002). At most one such card a day, below all existing morning problems (new group R5e), and the same card comes back after 7 days while the state lasts; from the 7th night of the state the step says `→ Trwa od tygodnia. Omów to z lekarzem.` A change in the good direction (AHI, PS, leak, breaks or flow limitation down, time with mask up) gives one attention line `Dobra zmiana: …`, at most once in 7 days. New rule kind `sk_odchylenie`, texts in `teksty.csv`, tests `test/testy_alerty_odchylen.cpp`.
- Minute ventilation (M-15) and tidal volume (M-14) in the night record: medians of the `MinVent` and `TidVol` channels from the AirBridge JSON report, from the PLD file for nights computed from session files, or from the STR statistics. The night file is now version 5 (`m14;m15` at the end of the record); the first start of the new firmware migrates files of versions 1–4 (empty fields at the end, a `.vN` copy of each file before the change), and older nights have empty values that do not enter the norm. M-15 is a companion signal of the infection card S-K-30 in every mode, also ASVAuto; a night without M-15 skips the signal. `noc_json` accepts `m14` and `m15`; `tools/kalibracja/noc_panelu.sh` compares them (M-14 ≤ 20 mL, M-15 ≤ 0.3 L/min).
- Faster night result in the morning (R-DANE-61, R-DANE-62, R-PRZ-22). Dense window: around the usual end of therapy (median of M-18 + M-01 over the last 14 nights with therapy, at least 3 such nights) the panel checks every 5 min from 45 min before to 45 min after, inside 05:00–11:00, until the night is complete (wake-up reason `okno_geste`). Repeat while AirBridge processes the files after therapy: a second check 2 min after a `/api/status` that shows processing, at most 5 a day (reason `powtorka_przetwarzania`). Button wake-up in the morning without the night: the button action is drawn first, then the panel downloads the night with the morning question decision, at most once per 2 min, only on the day screen and cockpit pages `n / 6`; a press during the download interrupts it and is executed as a queued press. No dense window and no button download after a failed attempt (no answer from AirBridge or Wi-Fi, rejected login), so days without data cost the same as before. Tests `test/testy_poranek_szybciej.cpp` and `test/testy_poranek_brzegowe.cpp`.
- `tools/wydanie/wydaj.sh`: a release with one command on the owner's Mac (branch `main` without local changes and equal to GitHub, green CI on that commit, next version number from the latest release tag, explicit `tak`, build and image check, publication, then a check that the latest release has the image and `wydanie.json`). `wydaj.py --nastepna` prints the next version number; after `--publikuj` it verifies the published release.
- Night history from the device summary file `STR.edf` (AirBridge v2.3.1 or newer, [ADR-0035](docs/decisions/0035-historia-nocy-ze-str-edf.md)): a new panel gets time with mask, AHI, leak and settings for every day the device remembers at the first morning download, without copying the device SD card. These nights have their own source `S`, rank below full AirBridge nights and card imports, and have no inspiratory pressure or pressure support. The night file is now version 4 (migration from version 3 changes the header only).
- Nights computed on the panel from the session files `PLD`, `EVE` and `CSL` ([ADR-0036](docs/decisions/0036-noc-z-plikow-sesji-edf.md)), with the same methods as `tools/kalibracja/noce.py`; this gives live pressure support (PS). New setting `noc_zrodlo` in `/konfiguracja.txt`: `porownanie` (default), `plik`, `json`. In the default comparison mode the night still comes from the AirBridge JSON report; at the end of the wake-up the panel computes the same day from the files and compares both within the WY9 tolerances. The result is kept in `/dane/porownanie.csv` (30 days) and shown on the hardware test screen U-05 (`Noce z plików: zgodne 7 z 7, …`). The panel never switches the source by itself.
- Firmware update from GitHub releases ([ADR-0032](docs/decisions/0032-aktualizacja-firmware-z-github.md), [ADR-0038](docs/decisions/0038-wydania-z-prywatnego-repo-i-token.md)): new screen U-07 "Aktualizacja" (↻ held 2 s on the settings page), check and install with SHA-256, board and partition layout check, restart and rollback to the previous version if the new one does not confirm itself; one check a day in the morning Wi-Fi window, optional automatic install (`aktualizacja_auto`, phone form section "Aktualizacje"), update icon in the day-screen footer. The panel contacts GitHub only when a release token has been entered in the phone form; the token stays in the panel memory, never in the image, log or report. The release repository is a build parameter (`REPO_WYDAN`); in this repository its default is `rafmyr/cpapblog-eink`, which has no releases with a ready-to-flash image (ADR-0033 item 6). Release image without secrets: PlatformIO environment `e1001_wydanie`, `tools/wydanie/wydaj.py`, `tools/wydanie/certyfikaty.py`.
- The Wi-Fi update window password, the local environment source and the weather coordinates are stored in the panel memory; `OTA_PASS` and the other values from `secrets.h` are only initial values, and the password can be changed in the phone form.
- Panel configuration in the serial backup: the `Z` dump includes `/konfiguracja.txt` with a SHA-256 line; `tools/prototyp/zrzut_plikow.py` saves it byte for byte (also `--tylko-konfiguracja`) and `tools/prototyp/wgraj_pliki.py` restores it together with the other files (T-WYM-92). The panel refuses configuration content that would break the dump format.
- Settings page U-01 (`6 / 6`) lists the current settings for reading (network, AirBridge, weather city, profile, humour, sound and quiet hours, hint, CO2 sensor, version) with a software status line below (R-KONF-55, R-KONF-46).
- Tools: `tools/kalibracja/noc_panelu.sh` computes nights with the panel code on a PC and compares them with the AirBridge report; reference generators `tools/kalibracja/noc_wzorzec.py` and `str_wzorzec.py`; synthetic session and STR files in `tools/kalibracja/syntetyczna.py`.

Questions, confirmations and long presses stop the queue, so nothing is answered on a screen the user has not seen.

- Night portrait page under ▶ also on an ordinary morning: after 30 min, ▶ on the second morning screen opens the portrait before K1 (footer `▶ portret`), and that ▶ does not start the day slot.
- Night portrait: a leak segment at the start of the window stays visible; the start mark then does not cut the ring.
- Time with mask going down (E-13, M-01 deviation state): the "3 of 5 nights" check now counts nights with therapy (at least 15 min), including nights shorter than 4 h, and `{x}` in the reason is the mean of those nights. Before, short nights were not valid nights, so a run of short nights could never trigger the state.
- Firmware update: the GitHub release file server now uses a Let's Encrypt certificate, so the panel rejected it while downloading `wydanie.json` and showed "no internet connection". The built-in root certificates now include ISRG Root X1 and X2 (`tools/wydanie/certyfikaty.py`, `src/certyfikaty_github.h`), and the log says when a release server certificate is outside the built-in set.
- Sleep start M-18 from the AirBridge JSON report was 1–2 h late: the device's local time was converted through the panel time zone. M-17 and M-18 now use one function for the report and for the session files (R-DANE-27).
- Panel with a few months of night history crashed on start (`Stack canary watchpoint triggered (loopTask)` while drawing the day screen S8) and entered the safe mode U-06 after three failed starts. The loop task stack is now 48 KB (was 24 KB).
- Internal RAM ran out with a long history: rule-engine state and night records (mostly small map and set nodes) were allocated in internal RAM although the board has 8 MB PSRAM. Ordinary allocations from 8 bytes now go to PSRAM (`heap_caps_malloc_extmem_enable(8)`); the minimum free internal RAM went from 452 B to 162 KB. The boot log has a new line `pamiec start: PSRAM …`.
- `tools/prototyp/wgraj_pliki.py`: after `--reset` the first file could arrive truncated, because the panel does not read the serial port while drawing. The tool now waits until the screen is drawn and keeps the panel awake with `?` meanwhile.
- Calendar dates: `dataOk()` checks the number of days in the month and leap years, so `2026-02-31` is no longer accepted silently as 3 March. After the update a date outside the calendar saved earlier by the form (for example `wymiana.maska=2026-02-31` in `/konfiguracja.txt`) is skipped with a log line and has to be entered again. Night records are not affected.
- The screen number restored from RTC memory is range-checked (`ekranZRtc()`); an unknown value opens the day screen.
- Atomic file write on the panel compares the size of the `.tmp` file on flash with the expected size before renaming it.

## 1.0.0-beta.1 (2026-11-06)

First public version. Beta for testing on a small number of panels; not a release for the general public.

### Features

- Morning day screen: the last night (time with mask, AHI, leak) against the personal norm of the last 30 valid nights; rotation of calm screens, streaks and milestones; "uncertain night" when the leak is too high to judge the night.
- Cockpit of six pages (`1 / 6` … `6 / 6`): 30-night trends with the norm, last night details, open matters, equipment, settings.
- Therapy modes `CPAP`, `AutoSet`, `ASV` and `ASVAuto` (AirSense 10 and AirCurve 10 through AirBridge v2.2.0 or newer); in ASV modes EPAP and pressure support instead of pressure.
- Deviation states per metric (enter after 3 of 5 nights, leave after 3 nights back in range) and context screens for leak, mask removals, short nights, bedroom conditions.
- Doctor patterns: a screen that says whether and how soon to talk to a doctor, with "I know" and "Discussed with the doctor" confirmations; other patterns are only collected for the report.
- A4 report for the doctor as PDF, downloadable with a QR code from the panel's local server for 15 minutes, or a cheat-sheet screen.
- Morning question "What was different yesterday?" after an unusual night and on a random control night; answers exclude nights with infection or sleeping away from home from the norm.
- Night screen "Dobranoc" from 23:30 to 05:00 and a day slot: airing window, tomorrow morning, equipment, sunrise, optional daily picture from the card.
- Mask and filter replacement reminders.
- Optional local environment source (outdoor weather, bedroom CO2) and optional weather and air quality from Open-Meteo for a chosen city.
- Configuration from a phone: the panel's own WPA2 setup network with QR codes, a form for home Wi-Fi and AirBridge, a settings form later from the settings page.
- Battery level on the test screen, a warning line at 15% and a "charge the screen" screen at 5%.
- Hardware test screen with a Wi-Fi service window for firmware upload in the local network.
- Day simulator and screen previews on a PC.

### Known limitations

- Only `ASVAuto` on an AirCurve 10 CS has been checked on a real device. The modes `CPAP`, `AutoSet` and `ASV` were checked on synthetic data only ([ADR-0030](docs/decisions/0030-wersja-1-dla-asv-cpap-i-autoset.md)); data fields of AirSense 10 in AirBridge are known from the AirBridge code, not from a device.
- Thresholds marked `[PRÓG]` are not calibrated yet.
- Uploading over Wi-Fi is locked until a panel is moved by cable to the 32 MB partition layout.
- Firmware images are not signed; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- An image built from the example secrets file can replace network settings on a panel whose settings came from `secrets.h`; release images need empty secrets.
- A Qingping sensor is not read directly; bedroom CO2 comes only from the local environment source.
